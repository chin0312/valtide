# Limitations and evidence boundaries

1. **Development-test contamination.** The current chronological test period was inspected repeatedly. It is not a pristine lockbox for subsequent model selection.
2. **Closed-market truth is unavailable.** Pseudo-closures test causal hiding of an observable underlying; they do not prove fair value during a genuine weekend or exchange closure.
3. **Asset heterogeneity.** Strong SPYx/QQQx/AAPLx challenger behavior does not justify promotion on NVDAx/TSLAx.
4. **Non-stationarity.** REVIEW effectiveness changed materially across chronological blocks. Static fitted thresholds can decay.
5. **Point and interval quality differ.** Good P1a-C interval scores do not establish that P1a is the best point estimate.
6. **Runtime scope is narrow.** The deployable artifact in this handoff is NVDAx only; other assets have research outputs, not approved production bundles.
7. **Missing shared-storage URI.** The raw datasets are present in the supplied local source package, but the durable shared-storage destination and access policy remain a human decision.
